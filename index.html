    Straight answers.

    Still wondering about something? Text us. A real person on the crew answers.

    How much does it cost?

    It depends on how much space your stuff takes up on the truck. Text a photo and you'll have a price before anyone shows up.

    Do I have to carry anything out?

    No. Show us what's going and we carry it, whether it's in the backyard, the garage, or up a flight of stairs.

    How soon can you come?

    Text us and we'll give you the soonest time we have open. Tell us if it's urgent.

    Do I need to be home?

    Not always. If the items are outside or you can leave us access, we can handle it while you're out. Set it up over text.

    Is there anything you won't take?

    Hazardous stuff like paint, chemicals, fuel, and propane tanks usually can't go on the truck. If you're not sure, send a photo and ask.
